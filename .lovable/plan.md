

# The Nilgiri Root — Premium Cinematic Website

## Overview
A high-end, cinematic animated website for "The Nilgiri Root" frozen french fries brand. Dark, immersive design with scroll-triggered CSS/SVG animations, parallax effects, and a premium Apple-inspired aesthetic. Includes the uploaded FSSAI license as a real certificate and the brand logo throughout.

---

## Design System
- **Colors**: Deep Forest Green (#1B4332), Earth Brown (#5C4033), Golden Yellow (#DAA520), Dark backgrounds (#0A0A0A)
- **Typography**: Serif font matching logo for brand name, Inter for body text
- **Style**: Ultra-minimalist, large whitespace, cinematic dark theme with green/gold accents

## Navigation
- Sticky glassmorphism navbar with backdrop blur
- Brand logo (uploaded image) on the left
- Menu: Home, Products, Process, Certificates, About Us, Contact Us
- CTA: "Partner With Us" in golden accent
- Mobile hamburger menu with smooth slide-in

## Pages

### 1. Home — Cinematic Scroll Storytelling
Full-screen scroll-based animation showing potato-to-fries transformation using CSS/SVG animations triggered by Intersection Observer:

1. **Farm Harvest** — Sunrise gradient, potato rising from soil, "From the fertile soils of the Nilgiris"
2. **Washing** — Water splash SVG, "Carefully cleaned for purity"
3. **Peeling** — Rotating transform, "Precision peeling"
4. **Cutting** — Slicing motion into fry shapes, "Perfectly cut for consistency"
5. **Blanching** — Steam rising animation, "Blanched for the perfect texture"
6. **Frying** — Golden bubbling effect, "Lightly fried for crisp perfection"
7. **De-oiling** — Vibrating conveyor animation, "De-oiled for quality"
8. **Freezing** — Ice crystal particles + mist, "Instantly frozen to lock freshness"
9. **Packaging** — Product bag with cinematic glow, final CTA "Explore Our Products"

Below scroll story: product preview cards, "Why Choose Us" with stat counters, CTA section.
